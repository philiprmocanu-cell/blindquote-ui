Ranges

Banks price on bands anyway

They see the same credit and loan-to-value bands their own rate sheets use. They lose nothing they'd price on.

Guard

Over-asking is refused by code

A bank that asks for your exact income is blocked automatically and the refusal goes in your ledger.
